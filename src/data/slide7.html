Design rationale

Regulation, enforced by architecture.

Data never leaves Germany
  • Inference runs on our own servers in Nuremberg
  • No narrative sent to any external API
  • GDPR and EU MDR compliant by architecture
GDPR EU MDR
A human always makes the final call
  • System shows Top-5 suggestions with explanation
  • Reviewer accepts, corrects, or rejects
  • No autonomous coding — ever
EU AI Act
Every decision is on record
  • Candidates, scores, model version, reviewer action all stored — a complete record of all decisions (audit trail)
  • Auditor reads top to bottom — nothing reconstructed
  • Model pinned per case: output reproducible years later
