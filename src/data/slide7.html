Why retrieval first?
  • 27k PTs is too wide for any one model
  • Cheap broad recall, then precise rerank
  • Reranker + LLM only see ~20
?
Why a local LLM?
  • Narrative never leaves EU infra
  • Pinned model version, stored per case
  • No third-country transfer ever
?
Why human in loop?
  • High-risk system under AI Act
  • Top-5 shown, reviewer decides
  • No autonomous coding ever
?
Why score fusion?
  • Reranker and LLM can disagree
  • Single number to prioritize review queue
  • Not a calibrated probability — a triage index
?
Why persist everything?
  • Audit-grade reproducibility
  • Drift detection over time
  • Reviewer overrides feed quality loop
?
Why pin the model?
  • Output must be reproducible later
  • Auditor can re-run the exact code
  • Upgrades go through validation
