What we measure, and what a reviewer record shows.

Measurable now
  • Fallback rate: did the AI respond, or did it fall back to a default?
  • Speed: how many cases per hour, how long per case
  • Score spread: are ranking scores spread out, or all clustered together?
  • Signal detection: are any adverse events unusually common for one device type? (see right →)
PRR/ROR signals
PRR (Proportional Reporting Ratio) measures whether a specific problem appears unusually often for one device type compared to all other devices in the database. A PRR of 13 means the event is 13× more common here than in the background — a flag for the safety officer to investigate, not an automatic finding.
Adverse event Reports PRR
Device catching fire 85 13.47
Blood glucose false pos. 141 4.25
Manufacturing control iss. 37 4.84
Signal = flag for targeted review. Safety officer decides.
