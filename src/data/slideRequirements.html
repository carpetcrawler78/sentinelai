Why SentinelAI needs a staged design

Medical-device reports are noisy, terminology-bound, sensitive, and require human review.
Noisy free text
Reports mix device issues, patient outcomes, timelines, and vague wording.
Candidate retrieval
Standard terminology
Outputs must map to MedDRA terms, not free-form summaries.
Controlled vocabulary
Ambiguous meaning
Several terms can be plausible; the system must show uncertainty.
Top-K + confidence
Privacy & governance
Potentially sensitive reports should stay in the trusted environment by default.
Local-first processing
Auditability
Each suggestion must be reviewable: candidates, scores, version, and decision.
Logged pipeline
Scale & latency
A large model reading every full report would be slow, costly, and hard to control.
Staged architecture
Therefore, SentinelAI uses a staged human-in-the-loop design: retrieve candidates → rerank → suggest → review
