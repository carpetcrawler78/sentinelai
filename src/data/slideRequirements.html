Requirements

Requirements from the real-world problem.

SentinelAI has to handle noisy medical-device reports while staying traceable, local-first, and human-reviewed.
Noisy free text
Reports mix device issues, patient outcomes, timelines, and vague wording.
Needs structured extraction
Standard medical terminology
The output must map to MedDRA terms, not just free-form summaries.
Controlled candidate space
Ambiguous clinical meaning
Several terms can be plausible; the system must show uncertainty.
Top-K + confidence
Privacy & governance
Potentially sensitive reports should not leave the trusted environment by default.
Local/on-prem default
Auditability
Each suggestion must be reviewable: candidates, scores, version, and decision.
Logged pipeline
Scale & latency
A large model reading every full report would be slow, costly, and hard to control.
Staged architecture
Therefore, SentinelAI uses a staged human-in-the-loop design: retrieve candidates → rerank → suggest → review
