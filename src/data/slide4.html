Hard constraints

Three rules we cannot break.

1
No patient data outside servers in Europe
AI runs on our own server in Nuremberg, not in the cloud.
GDPR Art. 44 (transfer to third countries prohibited without legal basis)
GDPR Art. 9 (health data is special category, heightened protection)
GDPR EU MDR
2
Human always decides — system only suggests
Top-5 shown to reviewer; reviewer confirms or corrects.
EU AI Act Art. 6 + Annex III (medical device software = high-risk AI system)
EU AI Act Art. 16 (human oversight obligation for high-risk systems)
EU AI Act
3
Every decision logged, pinned, reproducible
Model version stored per case, full audit trail.
EU MDR Art. 83 (post-market surveillance system mandatory for manufacturers)
EU MDR Annex III (technical documentation must cover lifetime of device)
EU MDR
These three constraints shape every architecture choice on the next slides.
